# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone.

Be kind, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on
the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior is inconsistent with these expectations. To report a conduct
issue privately, contact the maintainers through a private channel listed on
the [Astar AS GitHub organization](https://github.com/astar-as). Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions and in public spaces where
someone is representing the project.
